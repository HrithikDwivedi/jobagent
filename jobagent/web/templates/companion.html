Application Companion

A browser extension that fills job application forms on any site (Workday, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, Taleo, company portals, and the Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby forms auto-apply handed back) from your confirmed details, saved answers and tailored resume. It works inside your own browser, so sign-ins, CAPTCHAs and multi-page forms are no problem; you review everything and press Submit yourself.

Set up in 2 minutes

  1. Download the extension (button above) and unzip it.
  2. In Chrome, Edge or Brave open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, press Load unpacked and choose the jobagent-companion folder.
  3. Create an access token below and paste it into the extension's settings (the JobAgent address is pre-filled: {{ server }}).
  4. Open any job application form and click the JobAgent icon → Fill this form.

What it does on the page

  • Fills name, contact details, links, location, work authorisation and sponsorship answers, notice period, salary and every saved screening answer.
  • Attaches your resume (the tailored one when the page matches a prepared application) and the cover letter.
  • Highlights fields: filled needs you{% if ai.allowed %} AI draft{% endif %}.
  • Save my answers learns the questions you answered yourself, so next time (and for auto-apply) they fill automatically.
  • I've submitted it records the application in JobAgent with the confirmation it saw.

It never presses Submit, never fills passwords, bank or ID numbers, never answers voluntary diversity questions with personal data (it picks "prefer not to say" when offered) and never solves CAPTCHAs. Your token is stored only in your browser and sent only to this JobAgent address.
